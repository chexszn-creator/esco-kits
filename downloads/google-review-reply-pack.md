## 1) Owner rules (tape near the desk)
- Reply to **every** new review within **24 hours** (ideal: same day).
- Thank first. Never argue in public.
- For bad reviews: acknowledge → own the fix → move offline (phone/SMS).
- Never post private customer details, pricing disputes, or blaming a tech by name.
- After a recovered bad review, ask (once, politely) if they’d update — never guilt.

Rule: *A replied review is marketing. An ignored review is a leak.*

## 3) 4-star / mixed replies
**A — Grateful + invite**
Thanks {{name}} — glad the job went well. If anything felt short of 5 stars, reply here or call us and we’ll make it right.

**B — Specific fix invite**
Appreciate the feedback, {{name}}. We’re always tightening up {{area}}. Want us to follow up by phone? Just say the word.

**C — Timing / wait**
Thanks for the honesty, {{name}}. We’re adjusting dispatch so wait times stay tight. Glad the work itself landed well.

**D — Communication**
{{name}}, thank you. Clear updates matter to us — I’ll share this with the office so the next visit is even smoother.

**E — Price sensitivity (no arguing)**
Thanks {{name}}. We aim for fair, transparent pricing and solid workmanship. If you want a callback on anything, we’re here.

**F — Soft recovery**
Grateful you took the time, {{name}}. One thing we could improve next visit?

## 4) 1–3 star recovery (public → private)
**A — Own it fast**
{{name}}, I’m sorry we missed the mark. Please call/text {{phone}} and ask for {{owner}} — I want to fix this personally.

**B — Scheduling fail**
Sorry about the delay, {{name}}. That’s on us. Reach {{phone}} and we’ll prioritize getting you back on the board.

**C — Workmanship concern**
{{name}}, we stand behind our work. Contact {{phone}} — if something isn’t right, we’ll come back and make it right.

**D — Tech / manners**
I’m sorry your experience with our team wasn’t what we expect, {{name}}. Please call {{owner}} at {{phone}} so we can address it directly.

**E — No-show / miscommunication**
{{name}}, that’s unacceptable on our end. Call {{phone}} — we’ll sort scheduling and get you taken care of.

**F — Billing confusion (stay high-level)**
Sorry for the confusion on billing, {{name}}. Reach {{phone}} and we’ll walk through it line by line — no runaround.

**G — After already fixed offline**
{{name}}, thank you for giving us the chance to make it right. We’re glad you’re sorted — and we’re here if anything else comes up.

**H — When details are disputed (still calm)**
{{name}}, thank you for the feedback. We’ve opened this internally. Please contact {{phone}} so we can resolve the remaining items offline.

**Escalation path (internal)**
1. Public reply within 24h using A–F  
2. Owner/office SMS or call same day  
3. Offer re-visit or partial make-good if warranted  
4. Only then (soft) ask if they’d consider updating the review
