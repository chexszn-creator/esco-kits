## E) One-page who-replies policy

### Who replies
| Review type | Who posts the public reply | Who owns the follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5★ | Owner, office lead, or trained CSR | Tech gets a “nice job” ping |
| 3–4★ | Owner or manager only | Manager calls/texts within 1 business day |
| 1★ | Owner or manager only | Owner phones same day when possible |

Office staff may **draft**; owner/manager **approves** anything under 5★ before posting.

### When
- **5★:** within 48 hours (same day is better)
- **3–4★:** within 24 hours + offline contact attempt
- **1★:** same day public reply + phone attempt; escalate to owner immediately

### Escalate rules
1. Mentions injury, property damage, or legal language → owner only; pause public detail; document.
2. Mentions a named tech → manager reviews before posting; no public blame.
3. Duplicate / fake-looking review → still reply professionally once; do not accuse in public.
4. Customer already got a refund/rework → reply thanking them for the chance to fix it; keep short.

### Never
- Argue, match tone, or post screenshots
- Offer pricing disputes in the public thread
- Ignore 1★ hoping it “goes away”

### Simple weekly habit
Every Friday: scan Google + Facebook. Zero unanswered reviews older than 48 hours.
