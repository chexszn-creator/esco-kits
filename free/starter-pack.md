## Part 1: Missed-call text-back

**The rule:** every missed call is a job walking to the next Google result. Text back in 60 seconds or less, and book the same day when you can. Whoever texts first usually wins the job.

### First reply (send within 60 seconds of a missed call)
> Hey, sorry we missed you. This is {shop}. Need service today or want to schedule? Reply 1 for today or 2 for later and we'll lock in a time.

### After-hours version
> Thanks for calling {shop}. We're closed right now but we got your call. Text us what you need and your address, and we'll reply first thing at {open_time}. Reply URGENT if it's an emergency.

### Soft bump (no reply after 2 hours)
> Quick check-in from {shop}: still need help at the house? Reply YES and we'll get you on the board.

### Booking confirmation
> You're set for {day} at {time}. Tech: {tech}. Address we have: {address}. Reply STOP to cancel.

### 30-second callback script (when they call back)
"Hey, this is {name} with {shop}. Saw you needed service. Are you dealing with no cooling, no heat, or a leak right now, or is it something we can schedule this week?"
Then: figure out the problem → offer 2 time windows → confirm the address → send the confirmation text.

### Phone setup checklist (any carrier)
- [ ] Business line rings phones that someone answers during open hours
- [ ] After 4 rings or busy → forward to a number that can send texts
- [ ] First-reply text loaded with your shop name
- [ ] Calendar link or "reply 1/2" path ready
- [ ] Owner gets a notification when a lead replies

## Part 2: Google review reply starter set

### Owner rules (tape near the desk)
- Reply to **every** new review within 24 hours.
- Thank first. Never argue in public.
- Bad review: acknowledge → own the fix → move it offline (phone or text).
- Never post private customer details, argue about prices, or blame a tech by name.
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review, and ask every customer (not only the happy ones).

### 5-star replies
**Short and warm**
> Thank you, {name}! Glad we could get {job} taken care of. If you ever need us again, we're one call away.

**Tech shout-out (first name only)**
> Thanks {name}! I'll pass this to {tech}. The whole crew loves hearing we made the house comfortable again.

**Repeat customer**
> {name}, thank you for choosing us again. Loyalty like yours keeps the trucks rolling.

### 4-star / mixed reply
> Thanks {name}, glad the job went well. If anything fell short of 5 stars, reply here or call us at {phone} and we'll make it right.

### 1-3 star recovery reply
> {name}, I'm sorry this wasn't the experience you expected from {shop}. I'd like to understand what happened and make it right. Please call me directly at {phone}. - {owner}, owner

### Review request text (send the same day as the job)
> Hi {name}, thanks for choosing {shop} today! If we did right by you, would you leave a quick Google review? It helps a small local business a lot: {review_link}
