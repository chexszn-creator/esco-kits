After-hours auto-reply texts for contractors

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Calls at 9 pm don't always mean an emergency. Sometimes the customer finally has a free minute. An after-hours auto-reply lets them know they've reached a real business and when to expect an answer, so they don't keep calling other companies.

If you don't offer emergency service

Thanks for contacting {Business}! We're closed right now and open again at {Time} {Day}. Reply with your name, address and what you need, and we'll reach out first thing.

If you do offer emergency service

Thanks for contacting {Business}. We're closed for the day. For emergencies (no heat in freezing weather, active leak, etc.) reply URGENT and our on-call tech will call you back. Otherwise we'll text you when we open at {Time}.

Weekend version

Hi! {Business} is off for the weekend and back Monday at {Time}. Send a quick description and a photo if helpful. You'll be near the top of Monday's list.

First-thing-in-the-morning follow-up

Good morning, {Business} here following up on your message last night. We have {Window1} or {Window2} today. Which works?

How to set it up

  • Personal cell: iPhone Focus modes and Android's Do Not Disturb can auto-reply to texts. Replies to missed calls depend on your phone and carrier, so test it.
  • Google Business Profile chat: if you've turned on messaging, you can set a welcome message there too.
  • Business phone apps / VoIP: most include a "missed-call auto-text" or "away message" setting.

The morning follow-up matters most. An auto-reply only helps if someone actually texts back first thing the next day.
