Appliance repair missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

In appliance repair, the expensive mistake is the second trip: you diagnose, find you don't have the part, order it, and come back. The missed-call text back is your best chance to avoid it. Get the brand, the model number photo and the symptom before you ever roll the truck.

1. First text

Hi, {Business} here. Sorry we missed your call. Which appliance is it (fridge, washer, dryer, dishwasher, oven/range) and what's it doing? A photo of the model/serial sticker helps us bring the right part.

2. Where to find the model sticker

The sticker is usually: fridge = inside on the side wall or under the crisper; washer = under the lid or on the door rim; dryer = inside the door frame; dishwasher = on the edge of the door; range = behind the storage drawer or oven door frame.

This one text saves a huge amount of back-and-forth, and customers appreciate it.

3. Refrigerator not cooling

Thanks. Is the freezer still cold and only the fridge is warm, or is everything warm? Is it making any clicking or buzzing? Meanwhile, move perishables to a cooler. Next opening: {Day} {Window}.

4. Washer leaking or not draining

Got it. If it's leaking, turn off the water valves behind the washer and leave the door/lid open. Front-load or top-load? We can be there {Day} {Window}.

5. Diagnostic fee and booking

Booked: {Day} {Window}. Our diagnostic visit is {Fee}, and it goes toward the repair if you go ahead. {Tech} will text when on the way.

Explain the diagnostic fee in writing before the visit. Customers who first hear about it at the door are the ones who leave angry reviews.

6. Part on order

Update: the {part} for your {brand} {appliance} is ordered and should arrive around {Date}. We'll text to schedule the install as soon as it's here.

Tips

  • Track first-time-fix rate. Getting the model number before the visit is the cheapest way to raise it.
  • Stay clear on warranty: if the appliance may still be under manufacturer warranty, tell the customer to check before paying you.
