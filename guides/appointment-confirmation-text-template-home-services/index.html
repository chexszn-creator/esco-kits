Appointment confirmation text templates for home services

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

No-shows and "I forgot you were coming" visits waste a whole time slot. Short confirmation texts at the right moments cut down on both. Here's a simple sequence.

Right after booking

You're booked with {Business}: {Day}, {Date} between {Window}. Address: {Address}. Reply C to change or X to cancel.

Day-before reminder

Reminder: {Business} is scheduled for tomorrow, {Window}. Please make sure someone 18+ is home and pets are secured. Reply C if you need to reschedule.

On the way

{Tech} from {Business} is headed your way, about {Minutes} min out.

Running late

Heads up: {Tech} is running about {Minutes} minutes behind because the last job ran long. Sorry for the wait. Still good for today?

Reschedule

No problem. We can do {Option1} or {Option2}. Which works better?

Why this sequence works

  • Easy replies (C / X) mean you hear about changes before the truck leaves.
  • The day-before text is when people remember they won't be home.
  • The running-late text is simple courtesy, and customers do mention it when they write reviews.
