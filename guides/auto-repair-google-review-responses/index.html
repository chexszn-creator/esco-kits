Auto repair Google review responses

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Most people already distrust car repair shops before they walk in, and they read your reviews looking for a reason to trust you. Negative auto repair reviews tend to follow a few patterns: "they tried to upsell me", "the price was higher than quoted", "the problem came back", "it took all day". The templates below handle each one without getting defensive.

5-star: honest mechanic

Thanks, {FirstName}! We'd always rather tell you a repair can wait than sell you something you don't need. See you at the next oil change. - {Shop}

5-star: fast turnaround

Thank you, {FirstName}! Glad we got your {Vehicle} back the same day. Drive safe!

1–2 star: "they tried to upsell me"

{FirstName}, thanks for the feedback. Our techs list everything they notice during an inspection so you're never surprised later, but we should have been clearer about what was urgent and what could wait. Only {Item} was needed to fix the problem you came in for. I'm sorry it felt like pressure. - {Owner}

Separating "urgent" from "can wait" in your reply also shows future customers how you work.

1–2 star: price higher than quoted

{FirstName}, I'm sorry the final bill was a surprise. Our policy is to get your approval before any work beyond the estimate. I'd like to go over the invoice and approval notes with you. Please call me at {Phone}.

1–2 star: comeback (problem returned)

{FirstName}, I'm sorry the {issue} came back. Our repairs come with a {X}-month / {Y}-mile warranty on parts and labor. Please bring it back and we'll diagnose it at no charge, and fix it if it's covered. Ask for {Name}.

Mixed: long wait

Thanks, {FirstName}. You're right, a 4-hour wait for an oil change isn't OK. We were short a tech that day and should have offered you a drop-off or a ride. We now give a time estimate at check-in.

Tips

  • Never post a customer's vehicle history or invoice in a reply.
  • Mention your warranty in negative replies. It's your best trust signal.
  • Ask for reviews at pickup, when the customer has the car back and the invoice matches the estimate.
