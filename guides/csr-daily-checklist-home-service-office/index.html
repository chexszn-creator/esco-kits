CSR and office manager daily checklist for home-service companies

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

In a small trade shop, the person running the office keeps the whole day together. When they're out sick or a new hire starts, the gaps show up fast: missed callbacks, unconfirmed appointments, unsent invoices. Writing the day down as a checklist fixes most of that.

Opening (first 30 minutes)

  • ☐ Check voicemail, overnight texts, web form leads and the answering service log
  • ☐ Return or text every overnight lead
  • ☐ Confirm today's appointments are on the board with tech, window and address
  • ☐ Send day-before reminders that didn't go out
  • ☐ Check parts arriving today and which jobs they're for

Through the day

  • ☐ Answer calls using the booking script; offer two specific time options
  • ☐ Text back any missed call within minutes
  • ☐ Update customers when a tech is running late
  • ☐ Log every bookable call and whether it booked
  • ☐ Lunch check: any open estimates to follow up today?

Estimate and invoice follow-up

Estimate follow-up (2 days after):
"Hi {FirstName}, it's {Name} at {Business}. Any questions on the estimate {Tech} gave you for {job}? Happy to help you pick an option."

Unpaid invoice (7 days after):
"Hi {FirstName}, friendly reminder that invoice #{Num} for ${Amount} is open. You can pay here: {Link}. Thanks!"

Closing (last 30 minutes)

  • ☐ Every job today closed out: notes, photos, invoice
  • ☐ Tomorrow's board built and reminders sent
  • ☐ Review requests sent for today's completed jobs
  • ☐ Callbacks and complaints logged and passed to the owner
  • ☐ Phones switched to after-hours mode

Weekly

  • ☐ Reply to new Google reviews
  • ☐ Unpaid invoices over 30 days list to owner
  • ☐ Restock office and truck paperwork
