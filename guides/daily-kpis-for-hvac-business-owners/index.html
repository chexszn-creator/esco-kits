Daily KPIs for HVAC and plumbing business owners

Updated 2026-09-28 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

You don't need a dashboard with 40 charts. Most owner-operators can run the business from a few numbers checked every morning. The goal is to catch problems while they're still small.

The short list

  1. Calls in / calls missed: leads you never got a chance to win.
  2. Jobs booked vs. capacity: are trucks full, empty or overbooked?
  3. Jobs completed yesterday: did the board actually get done?
  4. Callbacks / warranty returns: quality problems show up here first.
  5. Invoiced vs. collected: revenue you haven't collected yet can't pay anyone.
  6. Open estimates: work that's waiting on a follow-up.

Set your own traffic lights

Decide what "good" looks like for your shop. For example, missed calls: green = 0–1, yellow = 2–3, red = 4+. Base the thresholds on your own call volume, not on someone else's benchmark.

One fix per day

If a number goes red, pick one fix for today (not five) and text it to whoever owns it. Doing this every day is what makes the scorecard worth keeping.
