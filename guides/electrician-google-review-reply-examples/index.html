Google review reply examples for electricians

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Electrical reviews have a few themes that keep coming up: the job took longer because of a permit or inspection, the customer was surprised by the price of bringing old wiring up to code, or the electrician was praised for explaining things clearly. Your replies are read by the next homeowner trying to decide who to trust inside their walls, so they're really written for that person.

5-star: panel upgrade

Thanks, {FirstName}! A panel upgrade is a big job and we appreciate you trusting {Tech} and the crew with it. Your home now has room for {what they mentioned: the EV charger, the new kitchen, the heat pump}. If anything trips or looks off, call us directly. - {Owner}, {Business}

5-star: EV charger install

Thanks for the review, {FirstName}! Glad the charger is up and running. Tip for anyone reading: check with your utility, because some offer rebates or off-peak rates for home charging. Enjoy the drive. - {Business}

Adding one useful tip for future readers makes a reply worth reading, and it naturally includes words people search for, like "EV charger install".

4-star or mixed: delay from permit or inspection

Thanks, {FirstName}, and you're right that it took longer than we planned. The city inspection was scheduled {X} days out, and we should have told you that timeline at the start, not halfway through. We've changed how we explain permit timing on every quote. Glad the work passed and you're happy with it.

Don't blame the inspector or the city in public. Explain once, take responsibility for how you communicated it, and say what you've changed.

1–3 star: price surprise after opening the wall

{FirstName}, I'm sorry the final price wasn't what you expected. Once the wall was open we found {aluminum wiring / no ground / a damaged junction}, and code requires us to correct it before we can close up. We should have walked you through that before continuing. I'd like to go over the invoice with you. Please call me at {Phone}. - {Owner}

1-star: someone you can't find in your records

We take every review seriously, but we can't find a job under this name or address. If we worked for you, please call {Phone} so we can make it right. If this was meant for another company, we'd appreciate you updating it.

What electricians should never put in a reply

  • Don't describe a safety hazard at a specific customer's home in detail. It's their house and their privacy.
  • Don't argue about code requirements in public. Offer to explain on the phone.
  • Don't name the tech in a negative reply. Use first names only in positive ones, and only with the tech's OK.
