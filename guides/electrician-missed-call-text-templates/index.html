Electrician missed-call text templates

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Electrical calls are different from other trades because some of them are real safety emergencies. Your missed-call text should send those people to the right place first, then book everyone else.

First reply

Hi, this is {Shop} Electric. Sorry we missed your call. If you see sparks, smell burning or have a hot outlet/panel, call 911 or your utility first. Otherwise, what's going on? Outage, repair, or a quote for new work?

The safety line takes a few seconds to read and protects everyone. It also shows you know what you're doing.

Partial outage

Thanks. Is it one room/circuit or the whole house? Have you checked the breaker panel for a tripped breaker? If resetting doesn't hold, we'll get you on the schedule.

Quote / new work

Great. Can you text a photo of the area and your panel label? It helps us quote accurately. We have {Day} or {Day} for a walkthrough.

Soft bump

{Shop} Electric checking in: still need help? Reply with a good time and we'll lock it in.

Booked

Confirmed: {Day}, {Window}. {Tech} will text when on the way. Please make sure we can get to the panel.

Notes

  • Don't diagnose over text. Asking for photos is fine. Promising a fix before anyone has looked at it isn't.
  • Keep a note of which missed calls turned into jobs. Panel upgrades and EV chargers are large tickets, so even one recovered call can make a difference.
