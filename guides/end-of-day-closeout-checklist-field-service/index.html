End-of-day closeout checklist for field service businesses

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Most of the mess in a service business comes from loose ends that sat overnight: missing invoices, trucks without parts, customers nobody called back. A 15-minute closeout catches them before they pile up.

Jobs

  • ☐ Every job on today's board marked: done / rescheduled / needs return
  • ☐ Rescheduled customers have been told the new time
  • ☐ Photos and notes attached to each job

Money

  • ☐ Every completed job invoiced
  • ☐ Payments recorded
  • ☐ Unpaid invoices flagged for follow-up

Trucks and parts

  • ☐ Each tech lists parts used and parts needed
  • ☐ Restock order placed for tomorrow
  • ☐ Any vehicle issue reported

Customers

  • ☐ Unanswered calls and texts from today replied to
  • ☐ Review requests sent
  • ☐ Estimate follow-ups scheduled

Tomorrow

  • ☐ Board drafted with windows and techs
  • ☐ First appointments confirmed by text
  • ☐ On-call person confirmed

Tech closeout text (template)

EOD {Name}: Jobs done __ / __. Returns needed: ____. Parts used: ____. Parts needed: ____. Truck issues: ____.
