Garage door missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Garage door calls are often urgent. A car is stuck inside, the door won't close at night, or there was a loud bang and now nothing works. These callers book whoever answers first, so a fast, specific text back makes a big difference.

1. First text

Hi, {Business} here. Sorry we missed your call, we're on a repair. What's going on? Reply 1 = door won't open (car stuck), 2 = won't close, 3 = loud bang / spring broke, 4 = opener or remote, 5 = new door quote.

2. Broken spring (reply 3)

That loud bang is usually a broken spring. For safety, please don't try to lift the door or adjust the spring. They're under a lot of tension. We can be there {Today/Tomorrow} {Window}. What's the address, and is it a single or double door?

The spring warning is the most important line in this script. It protects the customer, and it tells them right away that you know what happened.

3. Car trapped inside (reply 1)

Understood, that's urgent. Is the door stuck closed, or is the opener just not responding? If there's power, try the wall button once. If it hums but doesn't move, stop and wait for us. Earliest arrival: {Time}. Address?

4. Door won't close at night (reply 2)

Thanks. First, check that nothing is blocking the two small sensors near the floor on each side and that their lights are on. Wipe the lenses. If it still won't close, we can come {Day} {Window}. Want it?

Sometimes the sensor tip fixes the problem and you don't get the job. That's fine. That customer remembers you and calls you for the spring later.

5. New door quote

Happy to quote! Can you send a photo of the door from the driveway and the width (single or double)? Any style in mind: insulated, windows, a color? We'll text options and a price range.

6. Booked

Booked: {Day} {Window} at {Address}. Please clear about 3 feet in front of the door inside the garage. {Tech} will text when on the way.

Tips

  • Say the trip/diagnostic fee in the booking text if you charge one.
  • Save door photos to the job so the tech brings the right spring size.
  • After-hours car-trapped calls are your highest-value misses. Make sure that path reaches a person.
