Google review response examples for contractors

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Writing a fresh reply every time gets old fast, and copying the same line on every review looks lazy. Keep a small set of varied replies and personalize each one with a name or job detail.

Five-star, short

  1. Thanks, {Name}! Glad everything went smoothly.
  2. Appreciate you choosing a local crew, {Name}.
  3. Thank you! We'll share this with {Tech}.
  4. Means a lot. Thanks for taking a minute to write this.
  5. Thanks, {Name}. Call us anytime you need us.

Five-star, mentions a detail

  1. Thanks for noticing the cleanup, {Name}. We try to leave every house the way we found it.
  2. Glad the {job} was done in one visit! Thanks for trusting us.
  3. So glad we could get you in the same day. Thanks, {Name}.
  4. Thanks for the shout-out to {Tech}. He'll be happy to hear it.
  5. Appreciate you, {Name}. Enjoy the new {item}!

Star-only (no text)

  1. Thanks for the 5 stars, {Name}!
  2. Appreciate the rating. Thanks for choosing {Business}.
  3. Thank you! Glad we could help.

Four-star

  1. Thanks, {Name}! If there's anything that would have made it a 5, we'd love to hear it at {Phone}.
  2. Appreciate the review. Glad the job turned out well, and we're always working to tighten up scheduling.
  3. Thanks for the feedback, {Name}. Happy to help again anytime.

Repeat customers

  1. Always good to see you again, {Name}. Thanks for sticking with us.
  2. Thanks for trusting us again with the {job}!
  3. Appreciate your loyalty, {Name}. See you at the next tune-up.
  4. Thanks for recommending us to your neighbor, too!

Tip: rotate through them so no two replies in a row look the same, and add one specific detail whenever the review gives you one.
