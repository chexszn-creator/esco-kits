Google review response examples for roofers and electricians

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Roofing and electrical jobs have their own review patterns. Roofers get reviews about storm work, insurance and cleanup. Electricians get reviews about safety, permits and price. Replies that mention the actual work read better to future customers than "Thanks for the 5 stars!"

Roofing: 5-star replies

Thanks, {Name}! Glad the crew kept the yard clean and the new roof came out the way you wanted. If you ever spot anything after a big storm, call us and we'll take a look.
Thank you, {Name}. Storm claims can be stressful, and we're glad we could help make the process easier. Enjoy the new roof!

Roofing: delay or weather complaint

Hi {Name}, you're right that the job took longer than planned, and we should have kept you better updated when the weather pushed us back. We're glad the roof is done, and we've changed how we send schedule updates. Thank you for your patience.

Roofing: nails or debris left behind

Hi {Name}, we're sorry about the nails left in the driveway. That's not our standard. Please call {Phone} and we'll send someone out to do a full magnet sweep at no charge.

Electrician: 5-star replies

Thanks, {Name}! Glad we could get your panel upgrade done and inspected without the hassle. Enjoy the extra capacity, and call us anytime.
Thank you, {Name}. Flickering lights can be a real safety concern, and we're glad we tracked it down quickly for you.

Electrician: price complaint

Hi {Name}, thanks for the feedback. Our pricing includes {licensed electricians / permits / warranty}, and we always aim to explain it before we start. If anything on your invoice wasn't clear, please call {Phone} and I'll go through it with you personally.

Either trade: 1-star recovery

Hi {Name}, I'm sorry about this. It isn't the experience we want anyone to have. I'm the owner and I'd like to make it right. Please call me directly at {Phone}.

Rules for both trades

  • Never mention insurance claim details, amounts or the adjuster in public.
  • Don't argue about code or safety in public. Take it offline.
  • Reply within a few days, and sign with a real first name.
