Hair salon Google review reply examples

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Salon reviews are emotional because hair is personal. A great cut gets a glowing review, and a color that came out brassy can get a 1-star with photos. People choosing a salon read the negative replies most carefully. They're checking whether you'll fix it if something goes wrong.

5-star: praising a stylist

Thank you, {FirstName}! {Stylist} loved working on your {cut/color}, and that {balayage} is gorgeous. See you in {6} weeks!

5-star: first visit

So glad you found us, {FirstName}! Welcome to the {Salon} family. We can't wait to see you again.

1–2 star: color didn't turn out

{FirstName}, I'm so sorry you're not happy with your color. You should leave loving your hair. We offer complimentary adjustments within {7} days. Please call or text {Phone} and we'll book you with {Stylist or our color lead} to fix it. - {Owner}

Mention your adjustment policy every time. It's the single most reassuring thing a future client can read.

Mixed: running late

Thanks for the honest review, {FirstName}. Waiting 30 minutes past your appointment isn't OK, and we're sorry. We're now building buffer time into color appointments so the whole day doesn't run late.

1–2 star: price higher than expected

{FirstName}, I'm sorry the price was a surprise. Pricing depends on hair length and how much product is used, and we should have confirmed the price with you before starting. Please call me at {Phone}. I'd like to make this right.

1-star: upset about a no-show fee

Hi {FirstName}, we understand fees are frustrating. Our 24-hour policy is in every confirmation text because a missed appointment means {Stylist} loses that income. If something unexpected came up, please call us at {Phone} and we'll talk it through.

Stay kind and explain who the policy protects (the stylist). Don't quote the customer's texts back at them in public.

Tips

  • Use the stylist's first name in positive replies. It helps them build their following.
  • Mention services people search for ("balayage", "men's fade", "keratin") naturally.
  • Reply within 48 hours. Salon clients notice.
