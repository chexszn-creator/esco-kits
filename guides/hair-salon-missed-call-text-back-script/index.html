Hair salon missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Stylists can't answer the phone with their hands in someone's hair, and front desks get swamped at peak hours. Salon callers mostly want one of three things: a booking, a specific stylist, or to know if you can fit them in today. A text back with your booking link and a waitlist offer covers all three.

1. First text

Hi! Thanks for calling {Salon}. We're with clients right now. Book any time here: {BookingLink}. Looking for a specific stylist or today's availability? Just reply and we'll check.

2. Same-day request

Let me check! What service (cut, color, blowout, beard trim)? We have {Time} with {Stylist}. Want it? If not, I can add you to today's waitlist and text if something opens.

A waitlist turns a "no" into a booking when someone cancels, and it's the cheapest way to fill late cancellations.

3. Color or big change (consult first)

Love it! For {balayage / big color change}, we start with a quick consult so {Stylist} can check your hair and give an accurate price and time. Could you send a current photo and an inspiration photo?

4. Booking confirmation with policy

You're booked: {Service} with {Stylist}, {Day} at {Time}. Please give us 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancels and no-shows may be charged {Fee}. Reply C to confirm.

Clear, friendly policy wording in the confirmation text makes enforcing it far less awkward later.

5. Day-before reminder

Hi {FirstName}! Reminder: {Service} with {Stylist} tomorrow at {Time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Can't wait to see you!

6. Rebook after the visit

Thanks for coming in, {FirstName}! {Stylist} recommends coming back in about {6} weeks. Want me to save {Date} for you?

Salon tips

  • Text from the salon's number, not stylists' personal phones, so client history stays with the business.
  • Track how many empty chairs late cancellations leave each week. See what it costs with the free no-show cost calculator.
  • Honor STOP requests and keep promotional texts separate from appointment texts.
