Daily checklist for home service business owners

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Owners of small service businesses tend to spend the whole day putting out fires. A fixed ten-minute routine at each end of the day stops most of them from starting.

Morning (before the first truck rolls)

  • ☐ Check missed calls, texts and web leads from overnight. Reply to every one.
  • ☐ Review today's board: every job has a tech, a window and the parts it needs.
  • ☐ Confirm first appointments by text.
  • ☐ Glance at yesterday's numbers (calls, booked, done, collected).
  • ☐ Pick one thing to fix today and tell the person who owns it.

Midday (5 minutes)

  • ☐ Any jobs running long? Warn the next customers.
  • ☐ New leads replied to?

Evening (end of day)

  • ☐ Every completed job invoiced.
  • ☐ Payments logged and follow-ups set for unpaid ones.
  • ☐ Tomorrow's board drafted.
  • ☐ Review requests sent to today's completed jobs.
  • ☐ Open estimates: who gets a follow-up tomorrow?

Weekly (30 minutes, same day each week)

  • ☐ Roll up the daily numbers.
  • ☐ Which number was red most often? That's next week's focus.

Print it, or put it in your phone's reminders. It only works if it's somewhere you'll see it every day.
