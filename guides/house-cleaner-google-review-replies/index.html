Google review replies for house cleaning businesses

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Cleaning reviews are personal because your team is inside the customer's home. People reading them want to know two things: is the work thorough, and can I trust these people? Every reply should answer both, especially the negative ones.

5-star that names a cleaner

Thank you, {FirstName}! We'll pass this on to {CleanerFirstName}. Reviews like this make her week. See you on the {next date / next visit}!

Use the cleaner's first name only, and only if the customer named them first. Never post a team member's last name or schedule.

5-star: deep clean or move-out

Thanks, {FirstName}! Move-out cleans are a lot of work and we're glad it passed inspection. Good luck in the new place. If you ever want a reset there, we'd love to help.

Mixed: missed spots

Thanks for telling us, {FirstName}. Baseboards and the inside of the microwave are on our checklist and should have been done. We'll come back this week to finish them at no charge, and I've added a final walk-through photo step for your home.

1–2 star: something was broken

{FirstName}, I'm so sorry about the {vase/frame}. Accidents happen, but you should have heard about it from us right away, not found it yourself. Please contact me at {Phone} so we can sort out a replacement or reimbursement. - {Owner}

Don't argue about value in public. Take it to the phone. If you carry bonding or insurance, you can mention that you have a process for damage, but don't promise a payout before you've looked into it.

1–2 star: late or no-show cleaner

{FirstName}, you planned your day around us and we let you down. I'm sorry. Our scheduling missed a sick call and nobody texted you, which is exactly what should have happened. Your next clean is on us, and we've added a backup-cleaner step for exactly this.

Trust and privacy concern

Thank you for raising this, {FirstName}. Your trust matters more than any single clean. I'd like to understand exactly what happened and look into it personally. Please call me at {Phone}. - {Owner}

Tips for cleaning companies

  • Never discuss what's inside a customer's home in a reply, even to defend yourself.
  • Mention the service type ("recurring", "move-out", "deep clean") in positive replies. It matches what people search for.
  • Ask for reviews after the 2nd or 3rd recurring visit, once the home looks its best.
