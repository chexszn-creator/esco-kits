House cleaning missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

House cleaning owners are usually cleaning when the phone rings, often with gloves on. Cleaning callers are price-shopping and want a number fast. If you text back within a minute with the three questions you need for a quote, you'll beat most competitors, many of whom never call back at all.

1. First text

Hi! This is {Name} with {Business}. Sorry I missed your call, I'm with a client. Happy to get you a quote by text. How many bedrooms and bathrooms, and is this a one-time clean, recurring, or move-out?

2. Quote reply (recurring)

Thanks! For a {X}-bed / {Y}-bath home, recurring cleans are {Price} every {week/2 weeks}. The first clean is a deeper reset at {FirstPrice}. I have {Day} at {Time} open. Want me to hold it?

Be clear that the first clean costs more. Not mentioning it is the most common reason for price complaints after the first visit.

3. Quote reply (move-out)

Got it. Move-out cleans for {X}-bed / {Y}-bath start at {Price} and include inside the oven, fridge and cabinets. Will the home be empty when we arrive? What's your move-out or inspection date?

Ask for the deadline. Move-out clients care more about the date than the price, and they're often happy to pay extra for a rush slot.

4. Entry and pets (after booking)

You're booked for {Day} at {Time}! Two quick things: how will we get in (you home / lockbox / garage code)? And any pets we should know about? We'll text when we're on the way.

5. No reply after 24 hours

Hi, {Name} from {Business} again. I still have {Day} open if you'd like a clean this week. No pressure, just reply with your bedrooms/bathrooms and I'll send a price.

Cleaning-business tips

  • Keep entry codes off the shared calendar. Store them in one secure place and delete them when a client cancels.
  • Same price, same words. Save your price replies as text shortcuts so every caller gets the same quote.
  • Recurring is the business. Mention the recurring price even to one-time callers. Many will convert.
