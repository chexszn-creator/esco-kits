How to ask customers for Google reviews (script for techs)

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

The best moment to ask for a review is when the customer has just told you they're happy. Your techs are standing there when that happens, so give them words that don't sound forced.

When to ask

  • The customer says something positive ("that was fast," "thank you so much").
  • The problem is clearly fixed.
  • You're wrapping up, and payment is settled.

When not to ask

  • The job isn't finished, or there's a callback coming.
  • There was any disagreement about price.
  • The customer seems rushed or unhappy.

The in-person script

Tech: "Glad we got that sorted for you. Quick favor: we're a small local company and Google reviews really help people find us. Would you be open to leaving a quick one? I can text you the link right now."

If yes: "Great, what's the best number?" (send the link)
If hesitant: "No pressure at all. Thanks again for having us out."

The follow-up text (send within the hour)

Thanks again for choosing {Business} today, {FirstName}! Here's the link if you have a minute: {ReviewLink}. It really helps our small team. {Tech}

Staying within the rules

Ask every happy customer the same way. Don't offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews, and don't ask only the people you expect to leave 5 stars. Google's policies prohibit incentivized and selectively solicited reviews.
