How to get more Google reviews for a small business

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Businesses that collect reviews steadily usually just have a routine they stick to. Here's a lightweight one that fits a small crew.

Step 1: Ask at the right moment

"Glad that's fixed! We're a small local business and Google reviews really help. Mind if I text you the link?"

Step 2: Send the direct link the same day

Thanks for choosing {Business} today, {FirstName}! Here's the review link: {Link}. It helps more than you'd think.

Step 3: Reply to every review

People are more likely to leave a review when they can see the owner actually reads them. A two-line thank-you is plenty.

Step 4: Put the link everywhere it naturally fits

  • Invoice and receipt footers
  • Email signature
  • A small QR code card left with the paperwork

Step 5: Track weekly

Week of: ______
Jobs completed: ___
Review requests sent: ___
New reviews: ___
Replied to all? Y / N
One thing to improve: __________

After a month you'll know how many requests turn into a review for your business, and whether your techs are actually asking.

Things that get businesses in trouble

  • Buying reviews or trading reviews with other businesses.
  • Offering discounts for reviews.
  • "Review gating," meaning you only send the link to customers you expect to be happy.
