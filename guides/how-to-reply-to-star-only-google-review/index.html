How to reply to a Google review with no text

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Many Google reviews are just stars with no comment. They're easy to skip, but replying shows future customers that you pay attention, and for low ratings it opens the door to fixing the problem. Keep these replies short. Writing a paragraph in reply to zero words looks odd.

5 stars, no text

Thanks for the 5 stars, {Name}! We appreciate you choosing {Business}.
Thank you, {Name}! It was great working with you. Call us anytime you need us.
Appreciate it, {Name}! Glad we could help.

Rotate between several versions so your profile doesn't show the same sentence twenty times.

4 stars, no text

Thanks for the review, {Name}! If there's anything we could have done to make it a 5, we'd love to hear it: {Phone}.

3 stars, no text

Thanks for the feedback, {Name}. We'd like to know how we could have done better. Please give us a call at {Phone} and ask for {Owner}.

1–2 stars, no text

Hi {Name}, we're sorry your experience wasn't what you expected. We couldn't tell from the review what went wrong, and we'd like to make it right. Please call {Owner} at {Phone}.

First check your records. If you can find the job, you might call the customer directly (privately) before or after replying.

Quick routine

  1. Once a week, open your Google Business Profile reviews.
  2. Reply to every new review, including star-only ones.
  3. Flag any 1–3 star review to the owner the same day.
