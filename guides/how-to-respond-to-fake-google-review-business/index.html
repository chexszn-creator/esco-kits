How to respond to a fake Google review

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Getting a 1-star review from someone you've never worked with is frustrating. The goal is not to win an argument in public. Your reply is for the next customer reading it, so it should make you look calm and reasonable.

Step 1: Check before you call it fake

  • Search your job software and invoices for the name, street and phone.
  • Ask your techs and office if anyone remembers the situation.
  • Check whether it could be a different business with a similar name.

Plenty of "fake" reviews turn out to be a real customer using a nickname or a spouse's account. Replying "you're not our customer" to a real customer goes badly.

Step 2: Report it

From your Google Business Profile, open the review and use the option to report it. Choose the reason that fits (for example, conflict of interest, off-topic or spam). Google decides whether it violates their policies. It can take time, and not every report is accepted, so reply in the meantime.

Step 3: Reply calmly

When you can't find any record

Hi {Name}, thanks for the feedback. We take every review seriously, but we can't find a job or call under this name or address in our records. If we did work with you, please call us at {Phone} and ask for {Owner} so we can look into it and make it right.

When it looks like the wrong business

Hi {Name}, we're sorry to hear about this experience. We couldn't find a record of working with you, and we wonder if this may be meant for another company. If we're wrong, please call {Phone} and we'll gladly look into it.

When it's a competitor or ex-employee (don't say so)

Thanks for your review. We don't have a record of this job. Our customers can always reach the owner directly at {Phone}, and we'd welcome the chance to talk.

What not to do

  • Don't accuse anyone by name or guess who wrote it.
  • Don't share customer details or job history in public.
  • Don't offer discounts or gifts for removing or changing reviews.
  • Don't flood your profile with reviews from friends to "push it down." That breaks Google's rules and can backfire.

The best long-term fix is a steady flow of honest reviews from real customers, so one odd review is easy to read past.
