How to respond to Google reviews as an HVAC company

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

People reading your Google profile look at your replies as well as your star rating. A short, human reply to every review shows them how you treat customers. Here's a system that takes about two minutes a day.

The rules

  • Reply to every new review within a day or so.
  • Thank them first, whatever the rating.
  • Mention the job type naturally (AC repair, furnace tune-up) when it fits. Don't cram in keywords.
  • Never argue in public, and never post customer details.

5-star examples

Thanks so much, {Name}! Glad we got the AC running again before the weekend heat. We'll pass your note on to {Tech}.
Appreciate you trusting a local shop with your furnace tune-up, {Name}. See you next season!

3-star example

Thanks for the honest feedback, {Name}. Sounds like the repair went fine but the wait for a time window was longer than it should have been. That's on us. We're working on faster scheduling, and if you'd like to talk about it, call me at {Phone}. {Owner}

1-star example

{Name}, I'm sorry. This isn't the experience we want anyone to have. I'd like to understand what happened and make it right. Please call me directly at {Phone}. {Owner}, Owner

Keep it short, take responsibility for fixing it, and move the conversation offline. Readers mostly want to see that you responded like an adult.

Make it a habit

Turn on review notifications in your Google Business Profile app and reply from your phone at the same time every day, for example right after the morning dispatch.
