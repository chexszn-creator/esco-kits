How to respond to a negative Google review as a plumber

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

A bad review hurts, especially when you think it's unfair. Your reply is written for the next hundred people who read it, more than for the reviewer. Use this four-step formula.

The formula: Thank → Acknowledge → Own the next step → Take it offline

Complaint: price was too high

Thanks for taking the time to share this, {Name}. I understand the invoice was more than you expected. We should have walked through the cost before starting, and I'd like to go over it with you. Please call me at {Phone}. {Owner}

Complaint: the fix didn't hold

{Name}, I'm sorry the {issue} came back. That's frustrating. Our work is backed by {your warranty terms}, and we want to come back out and fix it. Please text or call {Phone} and I'll schedule it myself.

Complaint: tech was late or didn't show

You're right to be upset, {Name}. Missing a window without a heads-up isn't okay. I've talked with our team about it. I'd like to make it up to you. Please reach me at {Phone}.

Review you can't match to any customer

Hi {Name}, we take every review seriously but can't find a record of this visit. Could you call us at {Phone} so we can look into it?

Never write

  • "That's not what happened."
  • Anything about the customer's home, payments or personal details.
  • Blame aimed at a named tech.
  • An offer of money in exchange for removing the review.

Once it's sorted out, you can ask once, politely, whether they'd consider updating their review. Don't push.
