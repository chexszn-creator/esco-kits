How to set up missed-call text back (any carrier)

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

You don't need expensive software to start texting back missed calls. Here are the options from simplest to most automated, with a checklist that works no matter which carrier you're on.

Option 1: Manual, free, start today

  1. Save your missed-call text as a keyboard text replacement (for example, type ;mc and it expands into the full message).
  2. Every time you see a missed call, open that number and send it. It takes a few seconds.
  3. At the end of the day, look through your call log for any you missed.

Option 2: Built-in business phone features

Many VoIP and business phone apps (plus some carrier business plans) can send an automatic text when a call goes unanswered. Look for settings called "missed call text," "auto-reply" or "away message." Test it from a different phone before you rely on it.

Option 3: Forward unanswered calls to a texting number

Conditional call forwarding (sometimes called "forward when busy / no answer") sends unanswered calls to a second number that can auto-text. The codes and menus differ by carrier, so check your carrier's help page or call support and ask for "conditional call forwarding on no answer."

Checklist before you go live

  • ☐ Your main line rings a phone that gets answered during business hours
  • ☐ After about 4 rings or busy, the missed call triggers a text (manual or automatic)
  • ☐ The text includes your business name
  • ☐ Replies go to someone who checks them
  • ☐ You've tested it from a phone that isn't yours
  • ☐ Customers can reply STOP to opt out

Compliance basics

Texting someone back about their own call is generally treated differently from marketing texts, but rules vary. If you use an automated platform, it may require business texting registration (10DLC in the US). Follow what the platform tells you and don't put inquiry numbers into promo lists without consent.
