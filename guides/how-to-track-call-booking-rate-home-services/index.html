How to track call booking rate for a home-service business

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Booking rate is one of the most useful numbers a trade shop owner can watch: of the people who called needing service, how many ended up on the schedule? It tells you whether your phones are making money or leaking it, and it's simple to track by hand.

The formula

Booking rate = booked jobs ÷ bookable calls × 100

Bookable call = someone asking for service you offer, in your area.
NOT bookable = wrong numbers, vendors, spam, existing-job questions, out-of-area.

Taking out the non-bookable calls matters. If half your calls are vendors and robocalls, counting them hides what's really going on.

How to use it

  • Track it daily, judge it weekly. Daily numbers jump around. The weekly average is what to act on.
  • Compare to yourself. Industry "benchmarks" online vary a lot and often aren't sourced. Your own trend is the useful number.
  • Look at the "why" column. If "price" keeps showing up, work on how prices are explained on the phone. If "timing" shows up, look at your schedule capacity.

Phone script tweak that often helps

Instead of: "We can probably get someone out next week."
Try: "I can get a tech to you {Day} between {Window}, or {Day2} morning. Which works better?"

Offering two specific choices makes it easier for callers to say yes than an open-ended "when works for you?"
