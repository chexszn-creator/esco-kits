Google review reply templates for landscapers and lawn care

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Landscaping reviews are different from most trades because many of your customers see your crew every week. One skipped mow, one rut in the lawn or one gate left open can turn a three-year customer into a 2-star review. Most of these complaints are fixable. A calm reply plus a follow-up visit often gets the customer to update the review on their own.

5-star: weekly maintenance customer

Thanks, {FirstName}! The crew loves your street. Your yard is one of the best-looking on the route. We'll keep the edges sharp through the season. - {Business}

5-star: design or install project

Thank you, {FirstName}! The {patio / bed redesign / retaining wall} came together beautifully, and you were great to work with. First-season tip: water the new plants deeply twice a week until they're established. Enjoy it!

Mixed: missed visit after rain

Thanks for the honest feedback, {FirstName}. Rain pushed our whole route back that week, and you should have gotten a text telling you when we'd be there instead of having to guess. We've added a rain-day text for every customer. Your next cut is on us.

1–2 star: turf damage or ruts

{FirstName}, I'm sorry about the ruts by the {driveway/side yard}. That's on us. Our mower shouldn't have crossed wet ground there. We'd like to come back, fill and seed that area at no charge. Please call or text me at {Phone}. - {Owner}

1–2 star: gate left open / pet concern

{FirstName}, we're really sorry. An open gate is a serious problem when you have pets, and we're glad {pet} is safe. Every crew member now does a gate check before leaving each property, and your account is flagged "close gate" on our route sheet. I'd like to talk with you directly at {Phone}.

Irrigation: system stopped working after a visit

Thanks for letting us know, {FirstName}. Sometimes a head gets clipped during mowing, and we'll fix it at no charge if that's what happened. We'll come out on {Day} to check the zone. If it turns out to be a controller or valve issue, we'll explain the options before doing any paid work.

Landscaping reply tips

  • Answer route complaints with a system change (rain-day text, gate check), not just an apology. Neighbors on the same route read your reviews.
  • Seasonal words help: mentioning "spring cleanup", "aeration" or "fall leaf removal" in replies matches what people search for.
  • Ask at the right moment: request reviews right after a visible win, like a fresh install or the first cut of spring.
