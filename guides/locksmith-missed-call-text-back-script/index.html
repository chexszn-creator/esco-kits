Locksmith missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Someone locked out of their house or car calls whoever shows up first on their phone, and keeps calling until a person responds. A missed call is almost always a lost job unless you text back right away. Locksmiths also need to set expectations about price and ID verification up front, because those are the two biggest sources of complaints in this trade.

1. First text

Hi, {Business} here. Sorry we missed your call, we're on a job. Are you locked out right now? Reply 1 = house lockout, 2 = car lockout, 3 = lost car key, 4 = rekey/change locks, 5 = something else.

2. House lockout

We can help. Address? A tech can be there in about {X} minutes. Lockout service is {Price range}. Please have a photo ID with that address (or a lease or utility bill) ready. We verify before opening any door.

Putting the price range and ID requirement in writing builds trust, filters out bad actors, and protects you.

3. Car lockout

On it. Where's the car, and what's the year/make/model? Car lockouts are {Price range}. We'll need ID matching the registration. Is anyone or any pet inside the car? If so, please call 911 too.

4. Lost car key

We can make a new key on site for most vehicles. Year, make, model, and is it a push-start or a regular key? We'll send an exact quote. You'll need ID and proof of ownership (registration or title).

5. Rekey / lock change

Happy to quote. How many doors, and is this after a move-in or a lost key? Rekeying is usually cheaper than replacing if your locks are in good shape. Next openings: {Day} {Window}.

6. On the way

{Tech} is on the way in a {color} {vehicle}, arriving around {Time}. They'll show ID when they arrive.

Tips

  • Never quote only the "starting at" price by text. Give a realistic range.
  • Telling customers the tech will show ID builds trust in a trade where scams are common.
  • Night lockouts are high-value calls. Make sure after-hours misses go to someone's phone.
