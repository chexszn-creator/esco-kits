Missed-call text back script for HVAC companies

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

In HVAC, a missed call is often somebody with no heat or no cool who is scrolling to the next listing. A short text sent within a minute keeps the conversation with you while you're on a roof or under a house. The scripts below are written for the way HVAC customers actually call: most are urgent, some are maintenance, and a few are just shopping prices.

1. First reply (send within 60 seconds)

Hi, this is {Shop} Heating & Air. Sorry we missed your call, we're out on a job. Is this for no heat / no cool right now, or something we can schedule? Reply 1 for urgent, 2 for scheduling.

Why it works: it explains why you didn't answer, it doesn't try to sell anything, and a one-digit reply is easy to send from a hot attic or a cold kitchen.

2. If they reply "1" (urgent)

Got it. What's the address, and is the system running at all (fan on, no air / totally dead)? We'll get you a time window in the next few minutes.

3. If they reply "2" (scheduling)

No problem. We have {Day} morning or {Day} afternoon open. Which works better? Just reply AM or PM.

4. Soft bump (no reply after about 2 hours)

Quick check from {Shop}: still need help with the AC/heat? Reply YES and we'll get you on the board today if we can.

Send one bump. More than one starts to feel like spam, and carriers may filter repeated unanswered messages.

5. Booking confirmation

You're set for {Day} between {Window}. Tech: {Tech}. Address: {Address}. We'll text when we're on the way. Reply C to change the time.

Setup notes

  • Get consent right. Texting someone back about their own inquiry is normal practice, but add a way to opt out and don't add them to marketing blasts without permission.
  • Seasonal tweak: in heat waves, change the first line to "we're slammed with AC calls today" so people know what to expect.
  • Keep a log. Write down each missed call, whether you texted within a minute, and whether it booked. After a few weeks you'll have your own numbers instead of guesses.
