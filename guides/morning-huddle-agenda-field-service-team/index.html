Morning huddle agenda for field service teams

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

A morning huddle is a short stand-up before the trucks leave. Done well, it takes ten minutes and saves hours of "where's the part?" and "who's taking the callback?" calls during the day. Done badly, it becomes a 45-minute lecture. Here's an agenda that stays short.

The 10-minute agenda

0:00  Today's board (3 min)
      - Each tech: first job, rough route, anything unusual
0:03  Parts & equipment (2 min)
      - Who's picking up what, from where? Anything on backorder?
0:05  Callbacks & problem jobs (2 min)
      - Any return visits today? Who owns them?
0:07  Safety / customer note (1 min)
      - Heat, ice, attic work, a customer with special needs
0:08  One focus for today (1 min)
      - e.g. "text on the way on every job" or "photos on every install"
0:09  Questions, then roll

Rules that keep it short

  • Stand up. Start on time even if someone is late.
  • Same agenda every day. Repetition is the point.
  • Problems that need more than a minute get handled after, with only the people involved.
  • No coaching one person in front of everyone.

For remote crews

If techs take trucks home and don't come to the shop, run it as a 10-minute group call at a fixed time, or a group text where everyone answers the same three questions:

Morning check-in:
1. First job + ETA?
2. Any parts you still need?
3. Anything I should know about today?

Owner's prep (5 minutes before)

  • ☐ Day board up to date
  • ☐ Callbacks assigned
  • ☐ Parts orders confirmed
  • ☐ Today's focus picked
