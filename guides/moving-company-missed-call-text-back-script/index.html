Moving company missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

People planning a move call several movers in one sitting, often on their lunch break. Your crews are out moving and the office is on another line. The mover who replies first with clear questions and a fast quote usually gets the booking, especially at the end of the month when dates fill up.

1. First text

Hi, {Business} here. Sorry we missed your call. Happy to quote by text! What's your move date, moving from/to (zip codes are fine), and how big is the place (studio, 1-bed, 2-bed, house)?

2. Size and access questions

Thanks! A few quick ones for an accurate quote: Stairs or elevator at either end? Any large items (piano, safe, gun safe, sectional)? Need packing help or boxes?

Stairs, elevators and heavy items are what make a move run long. Ask about them now so the final bill isn't a surprise.

3. Photo walkthrough option

If it's easier, send a quick video walking through each room and we'll give you a firm-range quote within {2 hours}.

4. Quote and hold the date

For {size} from {A} to {B}: {X} movers + truck, about {Hours} hours, estimated {Range}. {Date} is still open. A {Deposit} deposit holds it: {Link}. Want me to reserve it?

5. End-of-month urgency (honest)

Heads up: the last weekend of the month fills fast, and we have {N} crews left for {Date}. Happy to hold one for you until {Time} tomorrow.

Only use urgency when it's true. Made-up scarcity is the fastest way to lose trust.

6. Day-before reminder

Hi {FirstName}! Your crew arrives tomorrow between {Window}. Please have parking near the door, keep valuables and medications with you, and finish packing anything we're not packing. Questions? Just reply.

Tips

  • Follow your state's rules on estimates and paperwork, and the federal rules if you do interstate moves.
  • Track quote-to-booking rate by how fast you replied. It's usually the clearest proof that speed matters.
