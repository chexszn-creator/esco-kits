New hire first-week checklist for trades businesses

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

A new tech's first week decides whether they stay and whether customers get consistent service. A simple day-by-day plan beats "ride with Mike and figure it out."

Before day one

  • ☐ Paperwork sent ahead (tax forms, direct deposit, policies)
  • ☐ Uniform, phone or app logins, tools ready
  • ☐ Ride-along partner picked and told

Day 1: Welcome and basics

  • ☐ Shop tour, where parts and tools live
  • ☐ How the day board works
  • ☐ Safety basics and vehicle rules
  • ☐ First ride-along (observe only)

Day 2–3: Ride-alongs

  • ☐ Watch how the partner greets customers, explains pricing and cleans up
  • ☐ New hire handles parts of the job with supervision
  • ☐ Practice the job closeout: notes, photos, invoice

Day 4: Lead with backup

  • ☐ New hire leads a simple call while the partner watches
  • ☐ Debrief in the truck: one thing done well, one to improve

Day 5: Review

  • ☐ 20-minute sit-down with the owner
  • ☐ What's clear, what's confusing, what's missing
  • ☐ Plan for week two (solo simple calls? more ride-alongs?)

Standards to say out loud

1. Text when you're on the way.
2. Shoe covers inside, leave it cleaner than you found it.
3. Explain the price before you start the work.
4. Photos before and after.
5. Close out the job in the app before you leave the driveway.
