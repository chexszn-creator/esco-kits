On my way text template for technicians

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Most customer complaints about home-service visits aren't about the repair. They're about waiting: "nobody told me when they'd show up." A short text when the tech leaves the last job fixes most of that, and it costs nothing. Below is a full set you can paste into your phone, your field-service app or a text-expander.

1. Day-before reminder

Hi {FirstName}, this is {Business}. Reminder: we're scheduled for {Day} between {Window} at {Address}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule.

2. On my way

Hi {FirstName}, {Tech} from {Business} is on the way. Expected arrival around {Time}. Please make sure we can get to the {equipment/panel/water heater}. See you soon!

Adding "make sure we can get to the..." saves the first ten minutes of every visit that would otherwise be spent moving boxes.

3. Running late

Hi {FirstName}, {Tech} here from {Business}. The job before yours is running long. New arrival time is about {Time}. Sorry for the wait. Reply if that no longer works and we'll find another time today or tomorrow.

Send this the moment you know you'll miss the window, not when you're already late. Giving the customer an out ("reply if that no longer works") keeps it polite and saves a wasted trip.

4. Arrived / at the door

Hi, {Tech} from {Business}. I'm outside now in the {color} truck.

5. Job done

All done at {Address}. Summary: {one-line work summary}. Your invoice/receipt: {Link}. Questions? Just reply to this text. Thanks for choosing {Business}!

Tips

  • Include the tech's first name. People open the door faster to a name they've been told to expect.
  • A photo helps. If your app supports it, send the tech's photo with the on-my-way text.
  • One sender number. Send everything from the main business number so replies don't get lost on a tech's personal phone.
  • Opt-out. These are service messages about a job the customer booked, but still honor "STOP" and keep them off marketing lists unless they've agreed.
