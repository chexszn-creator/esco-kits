Google review replies for painting contractors

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Painting reviews often include photos, both great before-and-afters and close-ups of a drip on the trim. Homeowners are choosing a crew they'll have in their home for days, so they read reviews looking for clean, respectful, finished on time. Your replies should reinforce those three things.

5-star: interior

Thank you, {FirstName}! That {color name} looks fantastic in the living room. The crew was glad to leave everything cleaner than they found it. Enjoy the new space!

5-star: exterior

Thanks, {FirstName}! Your house really stands out on the block now. We'll check in next year to see how the finish is holding up. - {Business}

Mixed: missed spots / touch-ups

Thanks for pointing it out, {FirstName}. Touch-ups are part of every job we do, and we should have caught those spots in the final walk-through. We'll be back {Day} to finish them. You'll walk the job with our foreman before we leave.

1–2 star: paint on floors or plants

{FirstName}, I'm sorry. Protecting your floors and landscaping is part of the job, and we didn't do it well. I'd like to see the damage myself and make it right. Please call me at {Phone}. - {Owner}

1–2 star: weather delays

{FirstName}, you're right that the job ran {X} days long. Exterior paint needs dry weather and the right temperature to cure properly, so we paused during the rain. But you should have gotten a text every morning with an update, and we've changed that.

Estimate dispute

Hi {FirstName}, I'm sorry about the confusion over the price. Our estimate covered {walls and ceilings}. Trim and doors were listed as an option. We should have gone over that line by line before starting. Please call me so we can review it together.

Tips

  • Mention the color or finish in positive replies. Homeowners search for "Sherwin-Williams Agreeable Gray painter" and similar.
  • Ask for a review at the final walk-through, while the customer is looking at the finished room.
  • Ask permission before posting before-and-after photos of a customer's home.
