Pest control Google review reply templates

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

The most common negative pest control review is some version of "they sprayed and the bugs came back." Often that's just how treatment works: activity can spike for a while as pests are flushed out. But the customer doesn't know that, and the next person reading the review doesn't either. Your reply is the place to explain it.

5-star: problem solved

Thanks, {FirstName}! Glad the {ants / mice} are gone. {Tech} found that {entry point} was the culprit. If you see any activity between visits, just text us and we'll come back at no charge. - {Business}

5-star: recurring customer

Thank you, {FirstName}! Three years on the quarterly plan. We appreciate you. See you next {season}.

Mixed / 1-star: "the bugs came back"

{FirstName}, we're sorry you're still seeing {pest}. With {roaches/ants}, some activity in the first {X} weeks can be normal as the treatment works, but you shouldn't have to wonder. We should have explained that at the visit. Re-service is included, so we'll come back on {Day}. Please call {Phone} if that time doesn't work.

Explain once, without lecturing, and always offer the re-service. Readers want to see that you come back.

1–2 star: smell or residue concern

Thanks for letting us know, {FirstName}. That's not the experience we want you to have. I'd like our service manager to call you, go over what was applied and where, and come back if anything needs attention. Please reach us at {Phone}.

Never post product names, application details or safety claims in a reply. Move it to a phone call with a licensed person.

Billing: charged after cancelling

{FirstName}, I'm sorry about the confusion with your plan. If you cancelled before the service date, you shouldn't be charged. Please call {Phone} and we'll check the account and refund anything billed in error.

Tips for pest control reviews

  • Mention the pest and the area in positive replies ("termite inspection in {City}"). It helps with local search.
  • Ask for reviews after the follow-up visit, once the problem is clearly solved, not right after the first spray.
  • Keep a short "what to expect after treatment" text and send it after every first service. It prevents the "bugs came back" review.
