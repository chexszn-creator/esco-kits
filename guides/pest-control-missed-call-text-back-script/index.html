Pest control missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Pest callers are often upset ("I just saw a mouse in the kitchen") and want to know someone is coming. They'll also call the next company if you don't pick up. A good text back calms them down, identifies the pest, and books the inspection.

1. First text

Hi, this is {Business}. Sorry we missed your call. We can help. What are you seeing? Reply 1 = ants/roaches, 2 = mice/rats, 3 = bees/wasps, 4 = termites, 5 = bed bugs, 6 = something else. A photo helps a lot!

2. Rodents

Thanks. Rodents are one of our most common calls. Have you seen droppings, heard scratching in walls/attic, or seen one? We'll do an inspection to find entry points. Next openings: {Day} {Window1} or {Window2}. What's the address?

3. Stinging insects

Got it. Please keep kids and pets away from the nest, and don't spray or knock it down yourself. That usually makes them aggressive. Where is it (eave, ground, inside a wall)? We can be there {Day} {Window}.

4. Bed bugs

Thanks for reaching out. You're not alone, and this is fixable. Please don't move mattresses or furniture to other rooms yet, because that can spread them. Can you send a photo of what you found? We'll schedule an inspection and explain treatment options and prep before anything is done.

Bed bug callers are often embarrassed. Use calm, practical language and save the treatment details for the inspection.

5. "Is it safe for my kids/pets?"

Great question. Your technician will go over exactly what we use, where we apply it and how long to keep kids and pets out of treated areas, before we start. You can ask anything on site.

Don't make blanket "100% safe" claims by text. Let the licensed tech explain the product label instructions on site.

6. Recurring plan offer (after first service)

Thanks for having us, {FirstName}! Most {pest} problems come back without follow-up. Our {quarterly} plan is {Price} per visit and includes free re-service between visits. Want me to set it up?

Tips

  • Photos first. They help you send the right tech and quote accurately.
  • Discreet vehicles: if you offer unmarked service, mention it in the bed bug text. Many callers care.
  • Track the funnel: missed call, text reply, inspection, recurring plan. The recurring plan is where pest control companies make their money.
