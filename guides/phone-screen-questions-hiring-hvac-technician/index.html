Phone screen questions for hiring an HVAC or plumbing technician

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

A short, consistent phone screen saves you from spending an hour in person with candidates who were never a fit. Ask everyone the same questions so you can compare fairly.

Opening (1 minute)

"Thanks for applying. This is a quick 15-minute call to see if it makes sense to meet in person. I'll ask a few questions, then you can ask me anything."

Experience

  1. What kind of work have you done most in the last two years (residential service, install, new construction, commercial)?
  2. Walk me through the last service call you ran from start to finish.
  3. What equipment or brands are you most comfortable with?

Licensing and logistics

  1. Which licenses or certifications do you currently hold? (For HVAC in the US, ask about EPA 608.)
  2. Valid driver's license? Comfortable driving a company vehicle?
  3. Are you available for an on-call rotation?

Customer skills

  1. Tell me about a time a customer was upset when you arrived. What did you do?
  2. How do you explain a repair price to a homeowner?

Reliability

  1. What would your last supervisor say about your attendance?
  2. What are you looking for that you don't have now?

What to listen for

  • Specific answers, not generalities.
  • Respectful talk about customers and past employers.
  • Questions back to you about the work itself.

Keep questions job-related. Don't ask about age, family plans, religion, national origin or other protected characteristics.
