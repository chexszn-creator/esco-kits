Plumber follow-up text templates

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Plumbing calls usually fall into two groups: "water is on the floor right now" and "I've been meaning to fix this." Your texts should work out which one you're dealing with quickly. Here's a set covering the whole job, from the missed call to the thank-you.

Missed call: triage first

Hi, {Shop} Plumbing here. Sorry we missed your call. Is water actively leaking right now? Reply YES or NO so we can prioritize you.

If YES

Okay. If you can, shut off the water at the main valve (usually near the water heater or where the line enters the house). What's the address? We'll get the nearest plumber a time to you ASAP.

If NO

Good. What's going on (drain, water heater, fixture, something else)? A photo helps. We'll send a couple of time options.

On the way

{Tech} from {Shop} is on the way, about {Minutes} minutes out. Please clear a path to the {Area} if you can. See you soon.

Estimate follow-up (2 days after quoting)

Hi {FirstName}, {Name} from {Shop}. Checking in on the {Job} estimate. Any questions I can answer? Happy to walk through options if the price or timing is the hold-up.

Post-job check-in (next day)

Hi {FirstName}, just checking the {Job} is holding up well. If anything looks off, text this number and we'll take care of it.

That last text is where a lot of plumbers get their review requests and repeat work. You're checking on the job, not selling, and the customer notices.

Tips

  • Tell people to shut off the water only if they can do it safely. Don't give instructions that could get someone hurt.
  • Save these as quick replies in whatever you text from (phone, Google Business messages, your field software).
