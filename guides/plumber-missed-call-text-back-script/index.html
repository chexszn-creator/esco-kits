Missed-call text back script for plumbers

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

A plumbing caller who reaches voicemail is usually standing next to the problem. If water is on the floor, they'll call the next plumber on Google within a couple of minutes. Your text back has two jobs: find out whether water is actively leaking, and keep them from dialing someone else while you finish the job you're on.

1. First text (send within 60 seconds)

Hi, this is {Business}. Sorry we missed you, we're on a job. Is water leaking right now? Reply 1 = active leak, 2 = clog/drain, 3 = water heater, 4 = estimate or other.

Numbered replies get answered faster than "how can we help?" because the customer can reply with one thumb while holding a towel.

2. If they reply 1 (active leak)

Got it. If you can, turn off the water now: the main shut-off is usually where the line enters the house (basement, garage or near the water heater), or use the small valve under the sink/toilet. {Tech} can be there around {Time}. What's the address?

Giving the shut-off tip first is the most useful thing you can do for the customer, and it signals you know what you're doing. Don't promise an arrival time you can't hit. Give a window and update it.

3. If they reply 2 (clog / drain)

Thanks. Is it one fixture (one sink or toilet) or several drains backing up at once? If several, please avoid running water or flushing until we look. Earliest opening: {Day} {Window}. Want it?

"Several drains at once" points to a main-line problem, which is a bigger ticket and may need a camera. Asking now lets you send the right tech with the right equipment.

4. If they reply 3 (water heater)

Thanks. Is it no hot water, or is it leaking? If it's leaking, shut the cold supply valve on top of the heater if you can reach it safely. Gas or electric, and roughly how old? A photo of the label helps us bring the right parts.

5. After-hours version

Hi, {Business} here. We're closed right now, but we got your call. Active leak or no water? Reply URGENT and our on-call plumber will call you back (after-hours rates apply). Otherwise reply with your address and we'll text you a time first thing tomorrow.

6. Booked confirmation

You're booked: {Day} {Window} at {Address}. {Tech} will text when on the way. Please clear access under the sink / to the water heater. Reply R to reschedule.

Plumbing-specific tips

  • State after-hours pricing up front. Surprise emergency fees cause most bad plumbing reviews, so say it in the text.
  • Ask for a photo. A water heater label or under-sink photo saves a second trip for parts.
  • Don't diagnose by text. Triage, then book. Quoting "that's probably a $150 fix" by text sets an expectation you'll have to walk back.
  • Count the recoveries. Tally how many missed calls turned into booked jobs each week. That number tells you if the system is paying for itself.
