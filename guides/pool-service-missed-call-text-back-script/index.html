Pool service missed-call text back script

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Pool techs spend the day outside on a route, and many calls come in while they're elbow-deep in a skimmer. Pool callers fall into four groups with very different value: a green-pool cleanup, a new weekly-service customer, an equipment repair, and seasonal opening or closing. Sort them in the first text.

1. First text

Hi, this is {Business}. Sorry we missed you, we're out on the route. How can we help? Reply 1 = pool is green/cloudy, 2 = weekly service quote, 3 = pump/filter/heater problem, 4 = opening or closing.

2. Green pool

We can fix that! A photo of the pool helps us quote. Roughly how big is it, and how long has it been green? Cleanups usually take {X} visits. First visit available {Day}.

3. Weekly service quote

Great. Weekly service in {Area} is {Price}/month and includes chemicals, skimming, brushing, emptying baskets and a filter check. We service your street on {RouteDay}s. Want to start {Date}? What's the address and is it chlorine or saltwater?

Mentioning the route day ("we're on your street Tuesdays") reassures the customer and keeps your route tight. Price new customers so they fit the route.

4. Equipment problem

Thanks. What's it doing: not turning on, making noise, leaking, or low pressure? A photo of the equipment pad and the pump label helps us bring the right parts. Next opening {Day} {Window}.

5. Opening / closing

We're booking {openings/closings} now. Is it an inground or above-ground pool, and does it have a heater or a safety cover? Open dates: {Date1}, {Date2}. Which works?

6. Route-day reminder (for new weekly customers)

Hi {FirstName}, {Tech} will be at your pool tomorrow ({Day}). Please make sure the gate is unlocked and pets are inside. We'll text a quick photo when we're done.

Pool-service tips

  • Sending an after-service photo builds trust and cuts down on "did they even come?" complaints.
  • Green-pool callers often become weekly customers. Offer weekly service in the cleanup's final text.
  • Track which missed calls become route customers. A route customer is worth months of revenue, not one visit.
