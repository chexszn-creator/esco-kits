Rain delay reschedule texts and SOP for roofing and outdoor contractors

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Weather delays are part of outdoor work. Customers mostly understand rain. What they don't like is finding out at 8am when the crew doesn't show. A simple routine and a few prepared texts take the stress out of it.

The weather routine

  1. Afternoon before: check the forecast for each job location. Mark each job green (go), yellow (watch) or red (move).
  2. Red jobs: text the customer that afternoon with the new date options.
  3. Yellow jobs: send a heads-up text and make the call by a set time in the morning.
  4. Morning: confirm go or no-go by {time}, text every yellow customer, and update the board and crew.
  5. Re-book: rescheduled customers get the first open slot, ahead of new jobs.

Heads-up text (yellow)

Hi {FirstName}, {Business} here. Rain is possible tomorrow at {Address}. We'll make the final call by {Time} and text you either way. No action needed for now.

Reschedule text (red)

Hi {FirstName}, with the weather forecast for {Day}, we're moving your {roof/paint/concrete} job so we can do it right. We can do {Option1} or {Option2}. Which works better?

Same-morning cancel

Hi {FirstName}, the rain came in heavier than forecast, so we're not starting at {Address} today. Safety and quality come first. You're at the top of our list. We'll text by {Time} with a new date.

Mid-job weather stop (roofing)

Hi {FirstName}, weather is moving in, so the crew is securing and covering the roof now and will stop for today. Everything is protected. We'll be back {Day} to finish.

Tips

  • Set a fixed go/no-go time and tell customers what it is when they book.
  • Keep the board current so the office knows which customers have been told.
  • Put the weather policy in your estimate or contract so it isn't a surprise.
