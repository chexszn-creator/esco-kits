Review request text message templates

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

A text with a direct link gets far more reviews than "find us on Google." Keep it short and personal, and send no more than one reminder.

How to get your direct review link

In your Google Business Profile (search your business name on Google while signed in, or open the Business Profile app), look for "Ask for reviews" or "Get more reviews." It gives you a short link that opens the review box directly.

Same-day (best)

Hi {FirstName}, thanks for choosing {Business} today! If you have 30 seconds, a quick Google review helps a lot: {Link}

Next-day

Hi {FirstName}, {Name} from {Business}. Hope everything's working well since yesterday. If you're happy with the job, would you mind leaving a short review? {Link}

From the owner

Hi {FirstName}, this is {Owner}, owner of {Business}. Thanks for trusting us with your {job}. Reviews from real customers are how a small shop like ours gets found. {Link}

One gentle reminder (3–5 days later, only if no review)

Hi {FirstName}, no worries if you're busy, just resending the review link in case it got buried: {Link}. Thanks either way!

Dos and don'ts

  • Do send the request to every completed customer, not only the ones you think loved you.
  • Don't offer a discount or entry into a drawing for a review.
  • Don't send more than one reminder.
