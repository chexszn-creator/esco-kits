Roofer missed-call text back scripts for storm and leak calls

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Roofing call volume isn't steady. After a hailstorm or high winds your phone can ring more in two days than in the previous two weeks, and most of those calls go to voicemail while you're on a roof. Storm callers are also phoning three or four roofers at once. The first company that replies with a clear next step usually gets the inspection.

1. Storm-week first text

Hi, this is {Business}. Sorry we missed your call. We're doing storm inspections in {Area} this week. Is water coming inside right now? Reply 1 = active leak, 2 = storm/hail damage inspection, 3 = repair or replacement quote.

2. Active leak

Sorry you're dealing with that. If it's safe, move valuables and put a bucket under the drip. Please don't go up on the roof. We can get a tarp crew out around {Time}. What's the address, and is the leak near a chimney, skylight or vent?

Telling homeowners not to climb up is good safety advice, and it shows you're the professional. The chimney, skylight or vent question tells your crew which flashing to check first.

3. Inspection / insurance caller

Thanks. We do free roof inspections and give you photos of anything we find. You decide what to do with your insurance. Our next openings: {Day} {Window1} or {Window2}. Which works? Please send the address.

Stay factual about insurance. Don't promise a claim will be approved or say you'll "cover the deductible". Many states regulate what contractors can say about claims and deductibles. Offer photos and an honest inspection, and leave the claim decisions to the homeowner and their insurer.

4. Follow-up after no reply (next morning)

Hi {FirstName}, {Business} again. Still want someone to look at the roof? We have {Day} at {Time} open on your street. Reply YES and it's yours, or STOP and we won't text again.

5. Inspection booked

Booked: roof inspection {Day} {Window} at {Address}. You don't need to be home if we can see the roof from outside. We'll text photos and findings after. Reply R to reschedule.

Roofing-specific tips

  • Turn on the storm script only when there's a storm. Keep a normal version for the rest of the year so it doesn't sound like you're chasing storms.
  • Say where you're working. "In {Area} this week" builds trust with homeowners who are wary of out-of-town crews.
  • Photos sell. Promising photos in the first text gets more inspections booked than talking about price.
  • Rain delays: see our rain-delay reschedule texts.
