Roofing lead follow-up text messages

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Homeowners looking for a roofer usually contact several companies. The ones who reply quickly and sound like a normal local business get the inspection. These scripts steer clear of pressure tactics and anything that sounds like a promise about insurance.

Missed call or web form

Hi {FirstName}, {Name} with {Company} Roofing. Sorry we missed you. Is this about a leak/damage, or planning a replacement? We can usually get out for an inspection within a few days.

Active leak

Sorry you're dealing with that. Is water coming in now? If so, put a bucket and towels under it and move anything valuable. What's the address? We'll see how soon we can get someone there to look and tarp if needed.

Booking the inspection

We can come by {Day} at {Time} or {Day} at {Time}. The inspection takes about {Duration}. Does either work?

Day-before confirmation

Reminder: {Name} from {Company} is coming tomorrow at {Time} for your roof inspection. No need to be on the roof with us, but it helps to be home for a quick walkthrough after.

Estimate follow-up

Hi {FirstName}, following up on the roof estimate. Any questions on materials or timing? No rush, just want to make sure you have what you need to decide.

What to avoid

  • Don't promise that insurance will pay. That's the insurer's decision, and in some states making that promise is regulated.
  • Skip "today only" pricing pressure. Homeowners are already on guard with roofers after storms.
