Google review replies for tree service companies

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Tree work is high-stakes. A big removal next to a house makes homeowners nervous, and the reviews show it. Positive ones praise the crew's safety and cleanup. Negative ones are about ruts in the lawn, debris left behind, or damage to a fence or roof. Readers are deciding whether to trust you with the 80-foot oak over their bedroom, so reply like it.

5-star: big removal

Thank you, {FirstName}! That oak was tight against the house, and the crew is proud of how cleanly it came down. Enjoy the extra light in the backyard. - {Business}

5-star: storm cleanup

Thanks, {FirstName}! Glad we could get the limb off the roof quickly after the storm. If you see any new cracks or leaning trees, call us. We're happy to take a look.

Mixed: lawn ruts from equipment

Thanks for the honest review, {FirstName}. Heavy equipment on soft ground can leave ruts, and we should have put down mats. We'll come back on {Day} to level and seed the area at no cost.

1–2 star: debris left behind

{FirstName}, I'm sorry. Full cleanup is part of every job we quote, and sawdust and small branches in your beds aren't acceptable. A crew will be back {Day} to finish it. Please call me at {Phone} if anything else needs attention. - {Owner}

1–2 star: property damage

{FirstName}, I'm truly sorry about the damage to your {fence/gutter}. We carry insurance for exactly this. Please call me directly at {Phone} so we can document it and get it repaired.

Mentioning your insurance reassures future customers. Keep the details of the claim off the public page.

Stump grinding question in a review

Thanks, {FirstName}! The stump wasn't part of the original quote. Grinding is an add-on, and we're happy to come back and grind it. Text us at {Phone} for a price.

Tips

  • Mention the species and service in positive replies ("oak removal", "crown thinning") for local search.
  • Ask for a review right after cleanup, when the yard looks its best.
  • Never argue about safety in public. Invite the reviewer to a phone call.
