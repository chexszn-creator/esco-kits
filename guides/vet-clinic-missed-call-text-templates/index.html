Vet clinic missed-call text templates

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Vet front desks get slammed at opening, at lunch and at close, which is exactly when new clients call. A text back keeps the pet owner talking to your clinic instead of the next one they find.

First reply (new or unknown number)

Hi, this is {Clinic}. Sorry we missed your call, our team is with patients. If your pet is having an emergency, please call {ER name/number} now. Otherwise, reply with your pet's name and what you need and we'll help you book.

Existing client

Hi {OwnerName}, {Clinic} here. Sorry we missed you! Is this about {PetName}? Reply with what you need (appointment, refill, question) and we'll get right back to you.

Lunch hour

Thanks for calling {Clinic}! Our front desk is at lunch until {Time}. Reply here and we'll respond as soon as we're back, or book online: {BookingLink}

After hours

{Clinic} is closed right now. For emergencies call {ER}. For appointments, reply with your pet's name and reason for visit, and we'll text you when we open at {Time}.

Booking confirmation

{PetName} is booked for {Day} at {Time} with {Vet}. Please bring any prior records. Reply C to change.

Notes for clinics

  • Always include emergency redirect wording. Never try to handle an emergency over text.
  • Keep medical details out of texts, and follow your practice's privacy policy.
  • Your PIMS stays the system of record. Texting is only there to get the appointment booked.
