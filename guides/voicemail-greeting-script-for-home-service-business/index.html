Voicemail greeting scripts for home service businesses

Updated 2026-09-30 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Lots of callers hang up as soon as the voicemail starts. A short greeting that tells them what happens next, especially that you'll text them, keeps more of them from moving on.

1. The "we'll text you" greeting

You've reached {Business}. We're on a job right now. Hang up and we'll text you back in a minute, or leave your name and address after the beep.

2. Emergency-aware

Thanks for calling {Business}. If this is an emergency, like an active leak or no heat, say "urgent" in your message and we'll call back first. Otherwise we'll get back to you today.

3. After hours

You've reached {Business} after hours. We open at {Time}. Leave your name and what you need, or text this number, and you'll hear from us first thing.

4. Solo owner-operator

Hi, this is {Name} with {Business}. I'm probably under a sink or on a roof right now. Text me at this number. It's the fastest way to reach me.

5. Seasonal rush

Thanks for calling {Business}. We're busier than usual with the {heat/cold}, but we're answering every message today. Leave your address and we'll text you a time window.

Tips

  • Keep it under 15 seconds.
  • Say your business name first so callers know they reached the right place.
  • Record it somewhere quiet. Truck noise makes you sound like you don't care.
  • Pair the greeting with a missed-call text so people who hang up without a message still hear from you.
