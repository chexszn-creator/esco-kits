What to text a customer after a missed call

Updated 2026-10-01 · AutoFlow Digital. Swap the {placeholders} for your own details.

Most people who reach voicemail don't leave a message. A text that shows up a minute later gets their attention while they still care about the problem. A good missed-call text does three things:

  1. Says who you are. The number may not be saved in their phone.
  2. Explains the miss in a few friendly words.
  3. Asks one easy question they can answer with a word or a number.

Six examples you can adapt

Plain and friendly

Hi, it's {Name} at {Business}. Sorry I missed your call! How can I help?

Two-option reply

Hi, this is {Business}. Sorry we missed you. Is this something urgent today, or can we schedule it this week? Reply 1 or 2.

When you're on a job

Hey, {Name} from {Business}. I'm with a customer right now and couldn't pick up. Text me what's going on and I'll get back to you within the hour.

After hours

Thanks for calling {Business}. We're closed for the night, but we open at {Time}. Reply with your name and what you need and you'll be first on our list in the morning.

Returning customer

Hi {FirstName}, {Business} here. Saw we missed your call. Is this about the {LastJob} or something new?

Price shopper friendly

Hi, it's {Business}. Sorry we missed you! If you're looking for a price, send a photo and a quick description and we'll give you a ballpark by text.

What not to send

  • Long paragraphs or your full service menu.
  • Links in the very first message. Some people won't open a link from a number they don't know.
  • "Did you still need something?" three times in one day.

Pick one or two of these, save them as text replacements or quick replies on your phone, and use them every time. Being consistent will do more for you than finding the perfect wording.
