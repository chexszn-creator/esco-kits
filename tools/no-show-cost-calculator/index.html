How the calculation works

Weekly loss = appointments × no-show rate × [(1 − refill rate) × average value + wasted cost]. Monthly = weekly × 52 ÷ 12. The wasted-cost field covers what a no-show costs even when you refill the slot, like a truck that drove across town or color mixed for a client who didn't show.

What reduces no-shows

  • Booking confirmation text sent right away, with the day, time window and address.
  • Day-before reminder with "Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule". Rescheduling is better than a no-show.
  • On-my-way text for field visits, so the customer is home and the gate is open.
  • A clear, friendly cancellation policy stated in the confirmation, not first mentioned when you charge a fee.
  • A waitlist so late cancellations get refilled.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal no-show rate?

It varies by industry, booking lead time and whether you send reminders. Track your own: count booked appointments and no-shows for four weeks and divide.

Do reminder texts reduce no-shows?

Reminders and easy rescheduling usually reduce no-shows, because many missed appointments happen simply because the customer forgot. Measure your own before and after.

Should I charge a no-show fee?

Many salons and some service businesses do. If you do, state it clearly at booking and in the confirmation text, and use judgment for genuine emergencies.

Is my data saved?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
